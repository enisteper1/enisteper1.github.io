Experiments and notes on search

  • Nov 2026 Should product fields get different BM25 weights?

    Takeaway: weighting the fields is what pays, not splitting them or indexing more text. Letting the name dominate added 3.4 nDCG points with no new model; equal weights gained nothing, and gluing all five fields into one document made search worse.

  • Nov 2026 Which tokenizer should BM25 use? 17 options on three datasets

    Takeaway: normalization matters more than the tokenizer library. A plain regex plus Porter stemming beat NLTK, spaCy, pretrained subwords and n-grams on all three datasets.
